# Opportunity Management in ERP.net

## Turn Potential Business into Sales

Once a potential customer's interest develops into a genuine business opportunity, your sales team enters a new stage of the customer relationship.  

Some customers may be discussing their requirements, others may be evaluating your products and services, while others may already be negotiating commercial terms with your sales representatives.  

Understanding the potential value of these deals, their likelihood of success, and the time and effort needed to materialize them helps your business make informed commercial decisions.  

Opportunity Management in ERP.net helps your sales teams organize, evaluate, drive, and track the deals with customers while providing your business with visibility into its prospective sales and revenue.
